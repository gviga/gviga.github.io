I am a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca, working at the intersection of machine learning and geometry processing.

My research focuses on developing neural architectures to understand and model correspondences between 3D shapes. I am passionate about leveraging artificial intelligence to enhance computational reasoning in geometry. Currently, I am visiting LiX at École Polytechnique near Paris, where I explore how geometric reasoning can advance our understanding of neural architectures.

Previously, I earned a Master’s degree in Applied Mathematics from the University of Milan.
